# Contributing to Praxis

Praxis is publicly source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

## Current contribution policy

External code, documentation, graph, agent-definition, and other copyrightable pull-request contributions are **not currently accepted**.

Issues, bug reports, design discussion, security reports, test results, and non-code feedback are welcome. Please do not attach a proposed patch or substantial replacement text unless Convergent Systems has explicitly requested it.

This temporary policy exists so that Praxis does not accidentally accumulate separately owned contributions before a deliberate contributor-license/copyright policy is adopted.

A future contributor agreement may reopen pull requests. That policy will be published explicitly before external copyrightable contributions are accepted.
